    Struct SpringParameters

    Describes the spring a constraint uses for its soft limits or its motor.

    Namespace: Evergine.Framework.Physics

    Remarks

    A frequency of zero makes the limit hard, which is the default: the constraint stops the bodies outright rather than pulling them back.
