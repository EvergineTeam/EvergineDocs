    Class SoftBody

    Makes the surface of a SoftBodyMesh deformable: cloth, jelly or an inflated ball, depending on the shape the mesh generates and on what is set here.

    Syntax
    public class SoftBody : Component, IDependencyObject
    Remarks

    This is the behaviour half of a soft body. The shape lives next door in SoftBodyMesh, which is also what draws it; this component reads that surface, hands it to the solver and gives it mass, stiffness and pressure. Without a mesh beside it there is nothing to simulate and no body is created.

    The entity transform provides the spawn pose, position and rotation both, and after creation the simulation owns the motion. When UpdatePosition is enabled the transform follows the body without interpolation, because the vertices are read at step rate and interpolating the transform but not the vertices would make the mesh swim against itself. The transform scale is baked into the simulated surface, the way a rigid body bakes it into its shapes, because the pose Jolt keeps for a body has no scale of its own; changing the scale rebuilds the body from its rest shape. A negative scale mirrors the surface. The scale of a child entity is read from its world matrix, which cannot tell a mirrored parent from an unmirrored one.

    Soft bodies do not take part in collision events or queries in this version: contacts against them are silently discarded rather than resolved to a RigidBody.

    Gets or sets whether every free vertex is tied back to its nearest pinned vertex with a long range attachment.     Not rendered by the generic inspector: a list of groups of indices is unreadable as numbered rows, and SoftBodyPanelEditor presents it as one line per group instead, built by selecting vertices in the viewport.

    Editing the list in place needs a NotifyPinsChanged() afterwards for the body to be rebuilt around it.

    Only meaningful on a closed surface.
