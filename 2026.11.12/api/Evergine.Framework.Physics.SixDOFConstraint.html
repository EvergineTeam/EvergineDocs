    Class SixDOFConstraint

    The general constraint: every one of the six degrees of freedom can be left free, locked, or limited to a range, and each one can be driven by its own motor.

    Remarks

    Anything the other constraints do can be expressed here, at the cost of being harder to set up and a little more expensive to solve. Reach for it when a joint does not fit any of the specific ones, and for the joints a Bullet generic six degrees of freedom constraint used to build.

    Every limit and every target on this component says where the body it lives on goes. The simulation underneath states the same things the other way round, as where the other body sits relative to this one, so every value crossing that boundary is negated and every pair of limits is swapped. Without that, asking this joint to move two metres to the right would move it two metres to the left, which is what it did before the mirroring was put in. The slider constraint carries the same note for the same reason.
