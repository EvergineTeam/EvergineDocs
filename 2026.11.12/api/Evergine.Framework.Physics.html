    Ties the rotation of two bodies together at a fixed ratio, the way two meshing gears turn.

    HeightFieldCollider

    A terrain built from a square grid of heights. Far cheaper than a triangle mesh for the same ground, both in memory and in query cost.

    HingeConstraint

    Lets a body rotate around a single axis, with optional angular limits, friction and a motor. A door, a wheel on an axle and a lever are all hinges.

    MeshCollider

    Builds a collision shape out of triangle geometry, either the meshes of the model on the same entity or geometry handed over from code.

    PlaneCollider

    A half space, cheaper than a box when all that is needed is a flat collision surface.

    PointConstraint

    Pins two bodies together at a point while leaving every rotation free, which is what a ball joint does.

    PrimitiveCollider

    Base class for collision primitives whose dimensions can be expressed either in local units or as a proportion of the model bounds.

    PulleyConstraint

    Runs a rope from this body up over a fixed point, down over another fixed point and onto a second body, so that one rises as the other falls.

    RackAndPinionConstraint

    Couples the rotation of this body to the sliding of another one, the way a pinion drives a rack.

    RayEmitter

    Debugging aid that casts a ray along the forward axis of its entity every frame, draws the ray and what it hits, and can push the dynamic bodies it lands on.

    RigidBody

    Turns an entity into a movable physics body, either kinematic or dynamic.

    SixDOFConstraint

    The general constraint: every one of the six degrees of freedom can be left free, locked, or limited to a range, and each one can be driven by its own motor.

    SliderConstraint

    Lets a body slide along a single axis while keeping its orientation, with optional limits, friction and a motor. Lifts, drawers and pistons are sliders.

    SoftBody

    Makes the surface of a SoftBodyMesh deformable: cloth, jelly or an inflated ball, depending on the shape the mesh generates and on what is set here.

    SoftBodyMesh

    The surface a SoftBody simulates, and the mesh that draws it.

    SoftBodyPinGroup

    A set of SoftBody vertices held in place, and optionally carried by another entity.

    SphereCollider

    Gives a sphere shape to a PhysicsBody.

    StaticBody

    Turns an entity into a physics body that never moves under simulation. Best suited for level geometry.

    SweepTestEmitter

    Debugging aid that sweeps the collider of its entity along the forward axis every frame and draws the path and what it hits.

    SwingTwistConstraint

    Holds two bodies at a point and limits how far one may swing away from an axis and how far it may twist around it. This is the joint a shoulder or a hip is built from, and what ragdolls are made of.

    TaperedCapsuleCollider

    A capsule with a different radius at each end, useful for limbs and bottle shapes.

    TaperedCylinderCollider

    A cylinder with a different radius at each end. Setting one of the radii to zero gives a cone, which Jolt has no dedicated shape for.

    TrackedVehicleController

    A vehicle steered by driving its two tracks at different speeds, the way a tank or an excavator turns.

    VehicleController

    Base class of the vehicles. Turns the body on this entity into a chassis driven by the VehicleWheel components found on its descendants.

    VehicleWheel

    One wheel of a vehicle. Lives on a child entity of the one holding the VehicleController, whose local position decides where the wheel is mounted.

    WheeledVehicleController

    A vehicle whose wheels steer and are driven through differentials: a car, a truck or a trailer.
