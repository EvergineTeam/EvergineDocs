    Class SwingTwistConstraint

    Holds two bodies at a point and limits how far one may swing away from an axis and how far it may twist around it. This is the joint a shoulder or a hip is built from, and what ragdolls are made of.

    Remarks

    The swing and the twist each have a motor, so the joint can be left to hang, spun at a speed, or held at a pose. Holding it at a pose is what turns a ragdoll from something that only falls over into something that can be driven towards an animation.

    TargetOrientation

    Gets or sets the orientation the motors drive towards while in Position.

    Declaration
    public Quaternion TargetOrientation { get; set; }
    Property Value
    Type Description
    Quaternion
    Remarks

    This is the rotation of the connected body relative to this one, measured in the space of the constraint rather than in the space of either entity: the identity holds the joint at the pose it was created in, whatever way its axes happen to point in the world. Driving a whole figure to an animated pose means writing one of these per joint, which is what makes a ragdoll follow an animation instead of only falling over.
