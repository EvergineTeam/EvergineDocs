    Remarks

    A collider does not simulate anything by itself. It contributes a shape to the closest PhysicsBody found upwards in the entity hierarchy, so a body can be made of several colliders placed on its own entity or on its descendants. When a body owns more than one collider the shapes are combined into a compound.

    Changing any dimension marks the owning body dirty; the shape is rebuilt once at the beginning of the next frame, so editing several properties in a row costs a single rebuild.
