    Remarks

    The outline is drawn in the default pass at LayerOrder, after the render layers with a lower order, so it covers them and is covered by the ones with a higher order. Entities that share an outline group are outlined as a single silhouette. The meshes of an outlined entity use Color and the ones of its descendants ChildrenColor, as Unity does.
