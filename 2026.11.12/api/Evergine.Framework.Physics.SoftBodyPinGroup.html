    Class SoftBodyPinGroup

    A set of SoftBody vertices held in place, and optionally carried by another entity.

    Remarks

    A pinned vertex has no mass as far as the solver is concerned, so nothing the simulation does moves it. That is the whole mechanism: a hanging cloth is a cloth whose top row is pinned, and a flag on a pole is the same thing with the pole named as the anchor.

    The vertices are grouped because the anchor is nearly always shared: a cloth hung from a bar is one bar and one row of vertices, not one bar repeated once per vertex. Naming it per vertex meant picking the same anchor twenty times and reading twenty numbered entries to check it.

    Which vertices a group refers to are indices into the geometry the SoftBodyMesh generates. Changing the shape or its tessellation renumbers those vertices, so groups made against the old numbering will land somewhere else.
