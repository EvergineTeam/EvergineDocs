    Class VehicleWheel

    One wheel of a vehicle. Lives on a child entity of the one holding the VehicleController, whose local position decides where the wheel is mounted.

    public class VehicleWheel : Component, IDependencyObject
    Remarks

    A wheel is not a body. It is a constraint the vehicle solves against the ground, which is why a car built this way stays stable at speed where four bodies on suspension constraints would not.

    Remarks

    The simulation combines this with the friction of whatever the wheel stands on as the geometric mean, so on ground with friction one half the effective grip is about seventy percent of the tyre value. Raising this is the way to stop wheelspin without touching the ground bodies.

    LongitudinalSlip
